Ball Detection Faceoff

How sure is each score?

The bar is the range the overall F1 lands in 90% of the time when the test videos with labelled balls are drawn again at random, so a model can get lucky or unlucky videos. Where two bars overlap a lot, the test can't tell those models apart.

Head to head

How often the row model beats the column model over 2,000 redraws of those test videos. 95% or more is a real lead; around 50% is a coin flip.

Confidence threshold

F1 if the app kept peaks above a different confidence. The app uses 0.5 (the dashed line). A peak on the curve to the left would be found by lowering it.

The details

Video by video

F1 on each test video at 0.5 confidence and 4 px. A model that wins overall can still lose whole videos.
