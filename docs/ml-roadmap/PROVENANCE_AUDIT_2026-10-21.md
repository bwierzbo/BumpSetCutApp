## Leakage findings

**Hard (0).** No two videos that share a file fingerprint or a confirmed sourceId/sessionId/venueId sit on different sides of a split. (There are no confirmed IDs yet, so only the fingerprint check could fire.)

**Soft (8).** Card and uploader overlaps. A clip card is how the owner filed a channel or outing, not confirmed recording, session or venue identity (D-009), so these are uncertainty to resolve, not proven leaks:
- The frozen holdout is not self-contained by card: `bch_onl_sun_land_onl_03` is `legacy_holdout` while its sibling `bch_onl_sun_land_onl_03_v2` (same card, same channel, likely the same beach) is `dev_val` and was scored in the Oct 16 test-set run. Whether that is the same venue is exactly what a confirmed `venueId` would settle.
- Three cards span `tune` and `dev_val` (`bch_onl_ovc_land_onl_02` ×4, `grs_onl_sun_land_onl_01` ×2, `ind_ele_dim_land_self_02` ×3). Tune and dev_val are on the same side of the train boundary, so this is not a train leak, but tuner settings were searched on some videos of a card while others of that card scored the detector.
- Uploaders spanning splits: Blake Seegers (dev_val + tune), Grace Qian (dev_val + legacy_holdout + tune), Dianne Gan (**dev_val + legacy_holdout + train**). An uploader is a channel, not a venue, so these are soft; the Dianne Gan case means one legacy_holdout video shares a channel with train videos, which Phase 0 had already noted.

**Info.** Seven `dev_val` videos have no exposure and share no card, uploader or file with train: `bch_extra_self_05`, `bch_extra_self_18`, `grs_extra_self_06`, `grs_extra_self_07`, `grs_extra_self_14`, `grs_extra_self_21`, `ind_extra_self_09`. They are the only existing recordings that could become `final_test` without new footage. They are album imports from the owner's own phone, so their venue is knowable but not recorded.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **The holdout sibling.** `bch_onl_sun_land_onl_03_v2` is in dev_val while `bch_onl_sun_land_onl_03` is legacy_holdout. Options: (a) move `_v2` into `holdout` in validation.json so the card is whole (it stays legacy, both were scored); (b) confirm a `venueId` for both and let the audit decide (same venue → hard, different → clear); (c) drop `bch_onl_sun_land_onl_03` from the holdout. Recommendation: (a).
2. **Final test.** Whether the seven unexposed dev_val videos become `final_test`. If yes, you confirm their `venueId`/`sessionId` first (they are your own recordings) and the implementer adds them to validation.json `finalTest`; from then on they are excluded from every package, score run and tuning. If no, the final test waits for new recordings.
3. **Tune vs dev_val on one card.** Whether cards must be whole across tune/dev_val as well (the three soft cases). If yes, the tune list changes and the Oct 12 tuner results are no longer comparable to new ones.
4. **Venue and session IDs.** How you want to assign them. The data model and `--set-provenance` are ready; nothing is inferred. A practical minimum: confirm `venueId` for the four legacy_holdout videos, the seven final-test candidates, and any card whose uploader spans splits (Dianne Gan, Grace Qian, Blake Seegers).
5. **Source IDs.** Whether the file fingerprint may be *confirmed* as `sourceId` in bulk for YouTube-origin videos (the fingerprint is the YouTube video ID, which does identify the original upload). This would be an explicit owner confirmation, not an inference; local-file fingerprints stay inferred.

## What changed in the code (summary; see the PR)

- `ClipSource` gains optional `sourceId`, `sessionId`, `venueId` (decodeIfPresent; nil is never written). Legacy project shapes (Test1-era cards, the single-`source` shape) and every session shape on disk still decode: `RallyLabTests/LegacyProjectDecodingTests`.
- `pipeline_tuning/validation.json` gains an optional `finalTest` list. Reserved videos are excluded from `MultiFramePackage`, `TrainingPackage`, the model-evaluation frame set, `PreTracker`, and every direct scoring entry (`--score-rallies`, `--diagnose-rallies`, `--render-pipeline`, the RallyLab pipeline button); the tuner refuses a reserved name in its tune/holdout lists. Reservation loading **fails closed**: a missing file means no reservations (legacy), an unreadable file aborts the operation instead of silently emptying the reserved set.
- Exposure is now written at the moment of use to an append-only `manifests/exposure-log.json` (package exports, test-set scoring, tuning rounds, and every direct rally-scoring run: `--score-rallies`, `--diagnose-rallies`, `--render-pipeline`, `--serve-report`, the RallyLab Pipeline and Compare tabs), so the history no longer depends on artefacts surviving; `manifest.csv` of single-frame (YOLO) packages is also scanned. Detector evaluation (`detector_evaluation`) and pre-tracking (`pre_tracking`) are logged too. A damaged log or previous manifest is never overwritten (new rows are parked in uniquely named sidecars that discovery still reads and the next write folds back) and makes manifest generation refuse until it is repaired. A video moved to another card keeps its history (log rows are carried to the new name; the previous manifest is joined by fingerprint) and its confirmed provenance IDs.
- New `DatasetSplitManifest` (versioned, hashed, exposure carried forward) and `DatasetAudit`; headless `--manifest`, `--audit-dataset`, `--set-provenance`.
- New `RallyLabTests` unit-test target (25 tests).
- One surface parser (`ClipID`) replaces two duplicated switch statements.
