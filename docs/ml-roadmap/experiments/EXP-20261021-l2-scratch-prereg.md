**Status: technical recipe approved by the owner on 2026-10-21 with three amendments ([D-019](../../decisions/DECISION_LOG.md#d-019)), applied in this revision: H1 is a paired comparison on identical data (§1), the label provenance is stated (§2), and only the first seed is authorised (§4). The recipe (§3–§5) is unchanged by the recovery. No training is authorised.** This record follows [PREREGISTRATION_L2.md](../../templates/PREREGISTRATION_L2.md).

## 1. Objective and hypotheses

Establish the first model of lineage L2 ([MODEL_LINEAGES.md](../MODEL_LINEAGES.md)): a ball detector with **no training, tuning or selection exposure** to the 16 lineage-holdout recordings, trained from independent initialisation on a package whose contents are chosen from the split manifest and verified by preflight.

- **H1 (paired comparison with the shipped L1 model on identical data; amended per D-019).** The L2 model and the shipped L1 model (`scratch_768_1014` = the bundled `ball_heat`) are scored by the **same scorer on the same windows**: the trainer's `score` (balls in play, hit = peak within 4 px at 512 width, tracked-rally windows) over the L2 package's val windows (dev_val + tune, the 13 recordings in §2, at 768×432 colour). The L1 checkpoint is scored with `train_heatmap_model.py <L2 package> --size 768 --color --score-only runs/heatmap/scratch_768_1014/best.pt --name l1_scratch_768_1014_on_l2val` (no training; its metrics.json records the checkpoint's SHA-256); the L2 run's `best` block comes from its own training. Both carry per-video 4 px tallies and the digest of the val windows they were scored on (every window record and every frame's bytes); `scripts/desktop_training/paired_compare.py <L2 metrics.json> <L1 metrics.json> --manifest manifests/manifest.json` refuses the pair unless the digests match, then draws the **same** units for both models 1,000 times (seed 0) to give ΔF1 = F1(L2) − F1(L1) with a paired 90 % interval, overall and per surface, for two resampling units: whole val videos, and **recording groups** (videos of one clip card, alias group, confirmed session or venue, or uploader are one unit, because `_v2`… variants and same-channel videos are not independent evidence). Among the 13 val recordings there are **5 recording groups** (Arbel Sports 1; Blake Seegers' four `bch_onl_ovc_land_onl_02` videos; Grace Qian's four grass videos across three cards; Carsten Lindemeier 1; the three-uploader `ind_ele_dim_land_self_02` card), so the group-level interval is the honest one and will be wide. **Exploratory, not a non-inferiority test (D-019 amendment, 2026-10-22):** ΔF1 and both intervals are reported with the preregistered reading — an interval that excludes zero names the higher model; an interval that includes zero means the two were *not told apart at this sample size*, which is **not** evidence of equivalence. No margin is preregistered; one may be adopted for a later run only if justified before any L2 result is seen. Recorded in advance: the L1 checkpoint was *selected* on a val set that contained these same recordings (plus the five legacy-holdout clips), so the comparison favours L1. The historical L1 numbers (F1 0.879–0.922, Oct 16 val set including the five legacy-holdout clips) are descriptive context only.
- **H2 (holdout generalisation, diagnostic for L1, valid for L2).** On the lineage holdout, the L2 model's detector score per surface is reported next to the L1 shipped model's score marked *exposed, diagnostic-only*. No hypothesis about their order is preregistered: the holdout has **no reviewed ball labels today** (§8), so this evaluation cannot run until Task B.2 produces them. The L2 run is still worth doing now because it fixes the lineage and its package; the holdout look happens once, later, by §8.

## 5. Development validation and checkpoint selection

Val = the package's val windows (dev_val + tune recordings above). Selection metric = the trainer's **F1 on balls in play at 4 px (512-width scale) over tracked-rally windows**, computed every epoch; the best epoch's checkpoint is `best.pt`. Two runs are told apart only when their 90 % video-resampling intervals do not overlap. No holdout number is used for any of this.

## 6. Metrics

Detector (trainer's `metrics.json`): precision, recall, F1 on balls in play at 4 px and 8 px; resting-ball recall; occluded frames neither hit nor miss; dead-time fire rate (windows with a fire, peaks per window). End-to-end rally metrics are **not** part of this run: they need the corrected raw-vs-padded scorer and B.2 boundary truth (roadmap Phase 1A/2), and the seven candidates have no trustworthy rally marks yet.

## 7. Reporting by surface and uncertainty

Per surface (beach / grass / indoor) and per clip card, with window counts; 90 % intervals from resampling whole val videos (the trainer's method) **and** from resampling recording groups (§1: 5 groups in all — beach 2, grass 1, indoor 2). With 13 val recordings in 5 groups, every surface is *indicative only*; the group-level interval is reported first.

## 8. Protected-holdout access and evaluation

The 16 lineage-holdout recordings have **no reviewed ball labels and no rally marks** (D-017 §3; `LINEAGE-HOLDOUT-SUMMARY`); the pre-tracking machine tracks are not ground truth. Therefore **no holdout evaluation is preregistered for this run.** When Task B.2 produces reviewed labels for them, a separate preregistration names the single L2 candidate to be evaluated, the command (`--score-heat` on a `--val-only`-style export restricted to the holdout, written by a lineage-aware exporter still to be built), and the per-surface / per-group report; that evaluation is logged as `detector_evaluation` on every holdout recording. The L1 shipped model's score on the same export is reported alongside, marked *exposed, diagnostic-only*. Repeated looks and any selection on these numbers are forbidden.

## 10. Success, failure and limitations

- **Success (the experiment, not the model):** the package's preflight passed and its digest was verified on the training machine before the run; the run completed with the preregistered recipe and seed; ΔF1 = F1(L2) − F1(L1) is reported with both paired intervals and the preregistered reading (§1), without an equivalence claim; the exposure ledger shows only `training_package Scratch-L2-…` for the packaged recordings and nothing for the 21 withheld. Whether L2 is *better, worse or not told apart* is the result, not a pass/fail.
- **Failure:** a protocol deviation — a failed preflight, a digest mismatch on the training machine, a ledger row on a withheld recording, a change to the recipe or seed, or any look at the holdout → the run is void and the package is deleted. An interval entirely below zero is a finding that sends the recipe back for examination on L2 data before any other L2 experiment, not a protocol failure.
- **Limitations:** (1) the L1–L2 comparison is paired on identical windows (§1) but favours L1, whose checkpoint was selected on a val set containing these recordings; the historical L1 numbers come from a different val set and are descriptive only; (2) the holdout cannot be evaluated until labels exist; (3) 144 train/dev recordings have unknown venue/session relations to the holdout; (4) the ball labels are human-verified and complete per rally (§2), but the *selection* of which rallies were tracked started from L1 proposals, so a rally the L1 model never proposed may be under-represented — a possible model-assisted rally-selection bias, noted, not a label-quality limitation; (5) broad generalisation claims still need the planned new-footage final test.
